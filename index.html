About Our School

Learning today. Growing for tomorrow.

Sri Sathya Sai School is located in the peaceful surroundings of Metturu, Kotturu Mandal, Srikakulam District, Andhra Pradesh.


Our school is committed to providing meaningful education while helping students develop knowledge, confidence, discipline, communication skills and strong moral values.


We believe that every child has potential, and our responsibility is to encourage and support them on their journey.
